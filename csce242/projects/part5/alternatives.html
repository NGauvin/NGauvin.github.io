Why Alternatives?

Traditional portfolios of stocks and bonds are exposed to the same economic forces — recessions, interest rate shifts, and market sentiment. Alternative investments often have lower correlation to traditional assets, providing true diversification.

THE DIVERSIFICATION PRINCIPLE
σ2portfolio = Σ Σ wi wj σi σj ρij

Portfolio variance depends on the correlation (ρ) between assets. When ρ < 1, combining assets reduces total risk without proportionally reducing expected return. This is the mathematical foundation of diversification.

Alternative Asset Classes

Real Estate

MODERATE RISK
Real Assets

Physical property — residential, commercial, industrial. Returns come from rental income and price appreciation.

Typical Return 6–10%
Liquidity Low

Commodities

HIGH RISK
Raw Assets

Raw materials — oil, gold, agricultural products. Driven by supply/demand and macroeconomic conditions.

Typical Return 3–8%
Liquidity High

Private Equity

HIGH RISK
Private Markets

Investment in private companies. Higher return potential than public markets, but capital is locked up 10+ years.

Typical Return 12–18%
Liquidity Very Low

Hedge Funds

VARIABLE RISK
Alternative Strategy

Pooled investment funds using sophisticated strategies — long/short, arbitrage, macro — to generate absolute returns.

Typical Return 6–12%
Liquidity Low

Crypto / Assets

VERY HIGH RISK
Digital Assets

Decentralized digital assets. High volatility, speculative in nature. Emerging asset class with evolving regulatory environment.

Typical Return Highly Variable
Liquidity High
