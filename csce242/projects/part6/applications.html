Worked Example: StableCo Inc.

A fictional mature consumer staples company with steady cash flows and a 20-year dividend history.

COMPANY DATA
Annual Dividend D1 $3.20
Dividend Growth Rate 5% / yr
Required Return 10%
Current P/E 18×
EPS (ttm) $6.40
Net Debt / EBITDA 1.8×
DDM Valuation $64.00
P = $3.20 / (0.10 − 0.05)
P/E Valuation $115.20
P/E 18× × $6.40
VALUATION SUMMARY
Average Intrinsic Value $89.60
Current Market Price $110.00
Margin of Safety -22.8%

At $110.00, StableCo appears to be trading above our estimated intrinsic value. There is no margin of safety at this price — a buyer is either paying a premium for quality or the assumptions need revisiting.
